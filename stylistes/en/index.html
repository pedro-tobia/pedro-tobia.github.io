Pedro Tobia · Artistic Director

I am the one who draws, and the one who is there when it is cut.

Born in Toulouse, I dressed artists in Paris before Montréal. It teaches you one thing fast : a garment either holds the body or it does not, and no styling hides the difference.

Today I draw RADICE for Maison Godefroi, I choose the materials, and I receive you myself at the showroom.

The house has been cutting by hand for twelve years. Pattern, cutting, assembly : all of it happens in our own ateliers, in Montréal. That is why there is no measurement we cannot make.

Your client stays yours.

I never contact them without you, and they go on no mailing list. You remain the only link, from the first fitting to the delivery.

The materials

Six leathers, three furs, three wools.

Leather, silk and cashmere from the finest tanneries and ateliers in Italy. One hundred per cent natural, no synthetics in the collection. Here is what you need to judge up close, at the scale your clients look.

And if nothing here is quite right, I draw : we have the house's leather inventory at hand, and I take those projects on myself.
