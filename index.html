Pedro Tobia · Artistic Director

I cut for one body at a time.

I was born in Toulouse, in the south of France. I came to clothing through people rather than through school — as a celebrity stylist in Paris, working independently with artists signed to Sony, Warner Music, Believe and Rec. 118. Dressing someone who will be photographed all night teaches you one thing quickly: a garment either holds the body or it does not, and no amount of styling hides the difference.

Today I am artistic director of Maison Godefroi, in Montréal, and creative consultant on its collections. I draw the pieces, I choose the materials, and I am in the atelier when they are cut.

The collection

RADICE — root, in Italian.

A root is what holds, and what you do not see. The collection is built the same way: the work is in the pattern, the seam and the lining, not in a monogram on the outside. Thirty-one looks were shown on 19 September 2026 at La Passerelle, in Montréal's Old Port, for the Héritage show.

We source on a tight circuit, on very precise points, and we judge a material on one criterion: purity. One hundred per cent natural compositions — merino wool, lambskin, cashmere, suede, silk. No synthetics anywhere in the collection. That is a constraint we chose, and it is the reason the pieces age the way they do.

Everything is cut and sewn by hand in Montréal. Ten working days from the measurements to the delivery — made to measure, or in the house's standard sizes.
