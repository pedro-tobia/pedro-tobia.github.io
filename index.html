Pedro Tobia · Artistic Director

I cut for one body at a time.

The house carries its own standard sizes. When they do not work, and on a defenceman’s shoulders they often do not, the pattern is cut on the man, at the same price. Every stage of the making happens in our own ateliers in Montréal, which is why there is no size we cannot cut.

Born in Toulouse, I dressed artists in Paris before Montréal. It teaches you one thing fast: a garment either holds the body or it does not, and no styling hides the difference. Today I draw RADICE for Maison Godefroi, choose the materials, and I am in the atelier when the pieces are cut.

The collection

RADICE, root in Italian.

A root is what holds, and what you do not see. The work is in the pattern, the seam and the lining, not in a monogram on the outside. Thirty-one looks were shown on 19 September 2026 at La Passerelle, in Montréal’s Old Port.

One criterion on materials: purity. Merino wool, lambskin, cashmere, suede, silk. One hundred per cent natural, no synthetics anywhere. Everything cut and sewn by hand in Montréal.
